# SOP draft: piece R2, year-end on a trial balance file

DRAFT FOR HASHIM'S REVIEW. For Hashim to copy into the Closebook SOP in the Notion SOP Library. Notion is not edited from
here. Source: docs/BRIEF.md, "Piece R2" (Hashim, Nov 19, 2026, version 4), merged Nov 19, 2026 (PR #105).

Applies to trial balance files: Concept's monthly QuickBooks clients, clients' own software and bookkeepers' files.
Bank-statement files do not change.

## What changes for staff

- Step 4 is now "Assign mapping": each client account goes to a master account (the standard chart), not to a GIFI line.
- GIFI is matched and checked in step 8 only.
- Monthly QuickBooks files now take entries in Closebook. The entries go back to QuickBooks through the client entry list.

## Steps

1. Step 2: type last year's closing retained earnings exactly as printed on last year's T2, line 3849. Show a deficit as a
   negative amount; it shows in brackets. Press Enter or leave the box to save.
2. Step 3: import the trial balance. For QuickBooks clients, also upload the Account List, so the account types and the
   accounts with no balance come in.
3. Step 4: assign each account with a balance (this year or last year) or an entry to a master account.
   1. Click "Accept last year's matches" first, if it shows.
   2. Check every "Name changed" suggestion before you accept it.
   3. Tick the accounts, then click Assign on the master account, or drag them onto it.
   4. If Closebook warns that the account types differ, confirm only if you are sure. The account then goes to the reviewer
      in step 8.
   5. Use Undo straight after a wrong Assign.
4. Assign these to 10900 Clearing: payment app clearing (CAD and USD), Undeposited Funds, commission cheque clearing and
   QuickBooks Opening Balance Equity.
5. Where several client accounts sit on one master account, pick in the master account window which one takes entries. This
   is optional. If left blank, the entry window asks.
6. Sign step 4 once nothing is left unassigned. To change a mapping after that, take back the sign-off first.
7. Clear Opening Balance Equity with one RJE to retained earnings, dated the first day of the year.
8. If QuickBooks still carries last year's dividends or prior period adjustments, move each one to retained earnings with
   one RJE, dated the first day of the year, for exactly last year's balance. Closebook refuses an amount that does not tie
   to step 2 and names both figures.
9. Make the year-end AJE, RJE and TJE in Closebook, on monthly files too.
10. Step 6: retained earnings at the start of the year must equal step 2's figure, and every Clearing account must be nil.
    If the tie fails, step 6 names the accounts still carrying last year's balance.
11. Download the client entry list (PDF or Excel). On monthly files it is titled "Entries to record in QuickBooks". If it says
    accounts are not yet assigned, finish step 4 first: net income is not final.
12. Record the entries in the client's QuickBooks. Then mark each reviewed entry "Booked in QuickBooks" in Closebook.
13. Do not import the trial balance again once an entry is marked booked. Closebook refuses it.
14. Step 8: match accounts to GIFI and settle the reviewer's list, including any "Line changed from last year".
