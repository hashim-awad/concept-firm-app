Applies to everyone at Concept who uses Studio. Benchmark: CaseWare Cloud, where Admin is a right, not a title.

## What changes for staff

- There is no Owner role. A role is a job title: Partner, Manager, Senior accountant or Accountant. An Admin can add more.
- Owner became Partner, with every right. Staff became Accountant.
- Admin is not a title. It is a right that comes with the role.

## What each title starts with

| Title | Area levels | Letters boxes | Closebook switches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partner | Edit on every area. This makes Partner an Admin. | T1 letters, corporate letters, Can issue Final and send for signing | All five |
| Manager | Same levels as the old Staff role, plus Closebook Edit. Exports: No access. | T1 letters, corporate letters | Prepare, Review |
| Senior accountant | Same levels as the old Staff role, plus Closebook Edit. Exports: No access. | T1 letters, corporate letters | Prepare, Review |
| Accountant | Same levels as the old Staff role, plus Closebook Edit. Exports: No access. | T1 letters, corporate letters | Prepare |
| A new role | Closebook Edit | None, unless the Admin ticks them | Prepare |

These are starting points. An Admin can change any of them on the role page. Check the role page for the current levels.

## What Admin means

- A person is an Admin when their role has Edit on both Staff accounts and Settings.
- An Admin creates roles, changes permissions and Letters boxes, turns switches on or off, and assigns people to roles.
- An Admin cannot give a level, a Letters box or a switch they do not hold, and cannot raise their own role.
- An Admin cannot change a role that has more access than their own.
- Some settings need Admin plus Edit on the area they guard, for example Pricing, Tax rates, Engagement terms, Clients or
  Closebook setup. The full export needs Admin plus Exports Edit.
- Every change to roles, permissions, switches and role assignments is recorded in the activity log.

## The five Closebook switches

Each role has five switches. A switch only works when the role has Edit on Closebook.

1. Prepare: "Work the steps and sign the preparer circle."
2. Review: "Sign the reviewer circle and add review notes." Review also lets the person read a file's History.
3. Partner sign-off: "Sign the partner circle (steps 1, 9 and 10)." This applies from piece R3a-2. Until then it does
   nothing.
4. Lock and unlock: "Lock a finished file, and reopen a locked file with a reason. Logged in History."
5. Remove others' sign-offs: "Take back a sign-off made by someone else. Logged in History." This covers preparer and
   reviewer sign-offs. Without it, a person can take back only their own sign-off.

Until piece R3a-2, the same person may still prepare and review a step. Studio labels it and logs it.

## The Final box

- The Letters box "Can issue Final and send for signing" decides who issues Final letters and sends quotes for signing.
- At start only Partner has it.
- Only an Admin can change the signature image and the signer names.

## The safety rule

- At least one active person must always keep Edit on Staff accounts and Settings (an Admin).
- Studio refuses any change that would remove the last one: a role edit, a role change, switching an account off or
  deleting a role.
- If Studio refuses a change for this reason, give Admin to a second person first, then make the change.

## How to give someone Review or Lock

1. Sign in as an Admin.
2. Open Admin, then Roles.
3. Decide whether the change is for everyone with that title or for one person.
   1. For everyone with the title: open the role.
   2. For one person only: create a new role (for example "Senior accountant, reviewer"), then assign the person to it under
      Team.
4. Check that Closebook is set to Edit on the role. A switch does nothing below Edit.
5. Turn on Review, or Lock and unlock, or both.
6. Save. Studio refuses the save if you do not hold the switch yourself.
7. Ask the person to open a dummy file and check they can sign the reviewer circle or see the Lock button.
8. The change is recorded in the activity log. Nothing else is needed.
